# How to Add MyDramaList Catalogs to Nuvio and Stremio

Turn your MyDramaList watchlist, K-drama Top 10, weekly picks and actor filmographies into Nuvio and Stremio catalogs. Free, no password, set up in a few minutes.

[Start setup](/)

## What YourDramaList does

YourDramaList turns MyDramaList into catalogs for Nuvio and Stremio. Your watchlist, a daily Top 10 for each country, hand-picked monthly Spotlight lists, weekly themed picks and the filmography of any actor or director show up as rows on your home screen, with posters, ratings and details.

## Set it up in four steps

1. **Connect your MyDramaList username.** Open YourDramaList and type your MyDramaList username. We only read your public watchlist, so we never ask for your password. You can also continue without an account.

2. **Pick your catalogs.** Choose from Spotlight, This Week's Picks, Top 10 Dramas and Movies Today, Because you watched, Browse All, your watchlists, public MyDramaList lists and actor or director filmographies. Pick a country to see Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Taiwanese or Thai titles.

3. **Choose posters and details.** Pick TMDB or MyDramaList details, a poster style, MyDramaList reviews, and whether to hide unreleased or completed titles.

4. **Install in Nuvio or Stremio.** Copy your install link. In Nuvio, add it as an add-on. In Stremio, paste it into the add-on search box and press Install. Your catalogs then show on the home screen.

[Start setup](/)

## Change your catalogs later

New lists arrive every week, month and day. To add them, tap Configure on the YourDramaList add-on in Nuvio or Stremio. Your setup opens with everything already selected. Add or remove catalogs, install the new link, then remove the old YourDramaList add-on.

## Questions

### Is YourDramaList free?

Yes. YourDramaList costs nothing to use.

### Do I need my MyDramaList password?

No. YourDramaList reads only public MyDramaList pages, so it never asks for a password.

### Can I change my catalogs later?

Yes. Tap Configure on the YourDramaList add-on in Nuvio or Stremio to reopen your setup with everything already selected, change it, and install the new link.

### Why does my watchlist show nothing?

Your MyDramaList watchlist needs to be public. Private lists stay private, so YourDramaList cannot read them.

### Does it work on TV?

Your catalogs show wherever you use Nuvio or Stremio with the same add-on installed, including Android TV.
